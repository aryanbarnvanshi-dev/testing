About R. K. Mission Public School

Located in Madansath, R. K. Mission Public School is a CBSE-affiliated village school focused on accessible, quality education and community engagement.

Mission

To inspire curious minds and foster lifelong learning through inclusive, student-centered education.

Vision

To prepare learners with the skills and values to thrive in a changing world.

Values

Respect • Curiosity • Resilience • Community
