Programs & Curriculum (CBSE)

R. K. Mission Public School follows the CBSE curriculum and serves young learners from Nursery through Class 10 with age-appropriate pedagogy and remedial support.

Early Years

Nursery, LKG and UKG — play-based foundations for language, motor skills and social development.

Primary (Class 1–5)

Foundational literacy, numeracy and environmental studies with regular assessments and remedial help.

Upper Primary & Secondary (Class 6–10)

CBSE-focused curriculum, value education, exams preparation and activity-based learning.

Admissions & Fees

Admission Process

  1. Visit school office to collect application form or download from the site.
  2. Submit completed form with required documents listed below.
  3. Admission confirmation after verification and seat availability.

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate (original + copy)
  • Aadhaar / UID of child or parents (if available)
  • Proof of residence (electricity bill / ration card / village certificate)
  • Recent passport-size photograph of child
  • A test will be taken for the admission to test ability of the student.
